Here's my critique to help the script hit the mark:

1. The opening monologue sets the scene well but could use a stronger hook or punchline at the end to really grab the listener's attention.

2. The trivia questions are creative and tie into the world/characters, but a few more accessible questions mixed in would make it more engaging for a general audience. Not everyone will get the ultra-specific inside joke references.

3. The password game sounds fun but the script doesn't provide any details on how it's played over the radio format. A quick 1-2 line explanation would help.

To tighten up the script, I would cut the sponsor break timestamps, as those can be handled in production and don't need to be in the main script.

But overall, it's a unique concept and the character names, quirks and world details come through clearly in an efficient word count. Polish up those couple points and I think you'll have a sharp, entertaining radio show script.
